CASE STUDY 03

Frontline Service Recovery Framework

Role: Operations & Service Design Lead

CHALLENGE

Frontline Failure Risks: Uncommunicated backstage policies led to recurring weekly service failures—culminating in asset custody mismatches, lost items, and mounting parent complaints.

METHODOLOGY

Systemic Root-Cause Audit: Partnered with on-ground leads to shadow live operations and trace breakdowns between compliance guidelines and ground-level volunteers.

100% Incident Eradication in 30 Days. Empowered frontline teams with active operational guardrails to resolve complaints autonomously with continuous service assurance.

CASE STUDY 04

Turning Saved Places Into a Trip

Role: UX Researcher (Course Project)

CHALLENGE

Saved Pins Are Not a Plan: Singaporeans who plan their own trips struggle to translate all their saved pins in Google Maps into a usable daily schedule for their trips.

METHODOLOGY

Journey & Competitor Audits: Paired competitor audits (Google Maps, Wanderlog, TripIt) with mixed-methods research to uncover two systemic pain points: The "Relay Race" (81% juggle 2+ tools) and the "AI Trust Gap".

Research findings
"81% of travellers didn't want another discovery engine—they were drowning in scattered pins and needed a sequencing bridge."
ECOSYSTEM SOLUTION

Organisation Over Discovery: Synthesised data into a HMW statement revolving around organisation. Validated top preferences: Pin Organiser (84%), Day-by-Day Scheduler (82%), Route Optimiser (80%).

STRATEGIC OUTCOME

The research pointed to a clear requirement: a native, one-stop feature within Google Maps that bridges discovery and execution by enabling users to seamlessly organize pins into itineraries.

Insights synthesised
Feature preferences & Problem Statement
WHAT I WOULD IMPROVE FURTHER

Ideating Solutions & Prototyping
Translate validated preferences into interactive Figma prototypes. Conduct usability testing to validate end-to-end trip creation and measure AI trust calibration.

CASE STUDY 05

The Thinking Belle: Multi-Agent AI Content Pipeline

Role: Workflow Architect (Personal Project)

"The Bottleneck - Single-prompt AI generation caused severe instruction bloat, requiring a decoupled multi-agent architecture."
CHALLENGE

The Manual Monolith: Scaling an educational CX video series failed under manual prompting. Single-chat generation caused severe context window pollution, tone drift, and hallucination loops.

METHODOLOGY

Multi-Agent Specialisation: Transitioned from bloated single-prompting to an all-in-one agent. Ultimately, modularising the pipeline into 5 specialized AI agents (Concept, Script, Visual, Audio, Metadata).

Phase 1: Unstructured Chat — Single-thread prompting led to rapid context drift, tone inconsistency, and prompt collision.
"Applying backstage service blueprinting to AI operations eliminated the human-friction bottleneck of manual text transfer."
ECOSYSTEM SOLUTION

Automated Command Center: Unified multi-chat workflows into an Airtable command center via Make.com webhooks, embedding structured human-in-the-loop review gates before triggering downstream Gemini agents.

STRATEGIC OUTCOME

5 Agents LLM Pipeline | 100% Format Lock
Full-stack webhook routing. Centralised multi-chat orchestration into a single workspace, embedding human review gates to enforce tone, creative direction, and output quality.

Full-stack webhook routing between Airtable, Make.com, Gemini API and Google Drive API.
